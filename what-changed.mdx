## 20 September 2026

- The Map tab and the strip on Today are on satellite imagery; a chip switches to streets. Fields are filled with their crop's colour and the latest Sentinel pass can be laid over each polygon.
- A sensor's row shows its last reading, how long ago it came and the day's sparkline. A silent gateway is one card, its sensors folded under it.
- The weather card opens on rain: the forecast's next 24 hours beside the nearest gauge's last 24 hours, each labelled.
- A spray takes the water volume, growth stage, LERAP category and buffer, and harvest interval; the plant protection product record prints them.
- A photo can go on a spray, a fertiliser application, a nitrogen line or a note.
- The pack card on the Compliance tab shows the cover.
- The field strip is two by two, each reading a figure with the week's line under it.
- Every tap answers; screens open on what they showed last time; the type scale, the cards, the row glyphs and the figures are new.

## 19 September 2026

- The compliance pack in the official layouts: Defra's field record sheets, the Tried & Tested nutrient plan, the Voluntary Initiative spray record, the manure management plan in Defra's order, the SFI pack on the agreement's terms, a draft EUDR due diligence statement; a cover with the inspection-ready checklist.
- Today groups the same rule across fields into one decision; a threshold crossing is announced in words, once.
- The NDVI archive since 2017 and the zone stability verdict.
- The assistant answers about the farm and cites its lines; crop protection questions go to the advisor.
- Modelled nitrogen release on the field; an Ecowitt station through its own gateway; the admin funnel.
- The welcome screen opens on the map around the phone.

## 18 September 2026

- The farm map with 28 feature kinds and the siting checks; RPA shapefile and GeoJSON import.
- Livestock, herd moves and the manure management plan; key dates on Today and by email; the measured-against-modelled chart.
- The share link for a paid pack; the watermarked page-image preview for every export.
