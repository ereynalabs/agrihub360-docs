---
title: "Your First Week"
description: "One thing a day for seven days, about twenty minutes each. By the end the farm has a record, a pack and a second person on it."
---

Each day is three tasks, each linked to its page, with a line on where you should be by the end of it. Do them in order, because each day builds on the one before. A farm without any hardware can still finish the week: day five may simply end with the app saying no probe is needed.

## Day 1: Put the Farm on the Map

- [Upload the RPA File](/how-do-i/map/upload-the-rpa-file), or [Draw a Field](/how-do-i/map/draw-a-field)
- [Set Up the Farm](/how-do-i/more/set-up-the-farm): the nation first
- Give each field its crop, from the field's page

**By the end:** Today fills itself in, and the inspection list on the Compliance tab shows what is already held.

## Day 2: Read Today

- [See the Week's Decisions](/how-do-i/today/see-the-weeks-decisions)
- [Read Where a Decision Comes From](/how-do-i/today/read-where-a-decision-comes-from)
- [Get the Email and the Push Before a Date](/how-do-i/today/get-the-email-and-the-push-before-a-date)

**By the end:** you know which field has a closed period and when it starts.

## Day 3: The Yard

- [Draw a Store, a Heap and a Ditch](/how-do-i/map/draw-a-store-a-heap-and-a-ditch)
- Read the siting card on each feature
- [Read the Inspection List and Fix a Line](/how-do-i/compliance/read-the-inspection-list-and-fix-a-line)

**By the end:** the risk map exists. Each store is checked, or says why it cannot be.

## Day 4: The First Record

- [Log a Spray](/how-do-i/records/log-a-spray), or [Log Fertiliser or Manure](/how-do-i/records/log-fertiliser-or-manure)
- [Add a Photo to a Record](/how-do-i/records/add-a-photo-to-a-record)
- Read the evidence mark on the row

**By the end:** one line on the record with a photo, written in the day, on the field.

## Day 5: A Reading

- [Is a Probe Worth It Here](/how-do-i/sensors/is-a-probe-worth-it-here)
- [Connect a Station](/how-do-i/sensors/connect-a-station) you already own, or [Claim the Sensor](/how-do-i/sensors/claim-the-sensor)
- [Place a Probe on the Field](/how-do-i/map/place-a-probe-on-the-field)

**By the end:** a measured number beside a modelled one, labelled as such. Or the app's verdict that the model is close enough on this field.

## Day 6: The Pack

- [Build and Share the Pack](/how-do-i/compliance/build-and-share-the-pack): the preview is free
- Read the cover as an inspector would: [Reading the Pack](/for-inspectors/reading-the-pack)
- Fill in the holding and business on [Set Up the Farm](/how-do-i/more/set-up-the-farm)

**By the end:** your CPH and SBI on every masthead, and the pack, watermarked, in your hand.

## Day 7: The People

- [Invite the Team and Set Roles](/how-do-i/more/invite-the-team-and-set-roles)
- [Hand a Decision to Someone](/how-do-i/today/hand-a-decision-to-someone)
- [Invite an Advisor](/how-do-i/more/invite-an-advisor), if there is one

**By the end:** ticks carry names. If you have an advisor, they see your farm on their own Today.
