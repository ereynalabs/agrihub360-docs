Every page in these guides uses only words the screen shows, or words on this list. A word in bold on a page is defined where it first appears; this page holds the ones that appear everywhere.

## The Record

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Modelled** | Worked out by the app from public data. The number says which model. |
| **Measured** | Read by a device at a time. The number says which device and when. |
| **Record** | One thing that happened on a field: a spray, a spread, a move, a harvest, a note. |
| **Evidence grade** | How strongly a record proves itself: **in the field** when it was written within the day, on the field, with a photo or a reading; **typed later** when it was not. |
| **Pack** | The compliance pack: every record an inspector asks for, as one download, with a cover. |
| **Regime** | One set of rules the farm answers to: NVZ, SFI, the spray record, Red Tractor. |
| **Key date** | A date the law sets for a field: a closed period, a heap's move-by date, a cutting period. |
| **Decision** | One line on Today: a rule, a window or a reading that wants something this week. |

## The Land

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Field** | An outline on the map with a crop. The unit everything else attaches to. |
| **Zone** | A part of a field you treat differently: a wet corner, a sandy headland. |
| **Stable** | Zones that keep their order year after year in the satellite archive. Only a stable field can export a rate plan. |
| **NDVI** | A satellite measure of how green a field is. Greener is a stronger canopy. |
| **Cover** | How much grass is on a field, in kilograms of dry matter per hectare. |
| **Residual** | The cover to leave when the herd moves off, about 1,500 kg DM/ha. |
| **Feature** | Something on the farm map that is not a field: a store, a heap, a ditch, a borehole. |
| **Siting check** | The distance the rules want between a feature and water, measured on the map. |

## The Rules

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Closed period** | The weeks each year when certain manures and fertilisers must not be spread on a field. Set by the nitrate regulations. |
| **N-max** | The most nitrogen a crop may get in a year, averaged over the farm. |
| **NVZ** | A nitrate vulnerable zone. Most of England is one; Wales and Northern Ireland are whole-territory. |
| **SSAFO** | The rules on where a slurry store, a silage clamp or a fuel store may sit, and how far from water. |
| **RB209** | The AHDB nutrient management guide. The nutrient plan uses its tables. |
| **MAPP number** | The approval number on a spray product's label. |
| **LERAP** | The buffer strip rule beside water when spraying. |
| **SFI** | The Sustainable Farming Incentive, England's agri-environment scheme. |
| **CPH** | The county / parish / holding number. What an inspector asks for first. |
| **SBI** | The Single Business Identifier, the number the Rural Payments service knows the business by. |

## The Weather

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Delta-T** | The gap between the air temperature and the wet-bulb temperature. Whether a spray will land and stay. |
| **Window** | The run of hours in which a job can be done: a spray window, a drilling window. |
| **GDD** | Growing degree days, the heat a crop has had. What the growth stage is worked out from. |
