**Before you start.** The field has two or more zones and a **Stable** verdict. [Draw Zones and Run the Stability Test](/how-do-i/map/draw-zones-and-run-the-stability-test) comes first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the rates">
    On the zones page, under the verdict, **Rate plan, nitrogen**. **Set the rates**: the product, its nitrogen content in percent, a flat rate for comparison, and a kg N/ha figure for each zone. **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export for the tractor">
    On a Stable field the button is there. On any other field the card says why it is not.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the terminal">
    **ISOXML** for John Deere, Fendt, Claas, Kverneland and Amazone: a TASKDATA zip. **Shapefile** for Trimble, Topcon, Ag Leader and any GIS. A CSV with the rates sits beside both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The file downloads, and the plan is written to the nitrogen record as one line with the rate by zone, so the N-max check sees it. Export again after a change and the same line is updated, not added to.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| No Export for the tractor | The verdict is not Stable | Run the test; a Not stable field cannot export |
| The terminal wants a product rate | The N content is blank | Edit rates and fill in the percent; the file then carries kg of product |
| Exports are locked | The farm is on the free plan | More, Billing |
