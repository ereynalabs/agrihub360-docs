<Steps>
  <Step title="Map, Add a field, then Draw one field">
    **Add a field** under the map offers three doors; **Draw one field** opens the map around the phone. **My location** recentres it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the corners">
    Tap the map at each corner of the field, three or more. The hectares appear as you go. Tap a dot to remove it; **Clear** starts again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and say what is growing">
    **Field name**, then the crop. Hectares are filled in from the outline; change them if the mapped figure is wrong. **Save field**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The field is on the Map in its crop colour and on Today the moment a rule applies to it. A field with a crop feeds the nutrient plan and the closed periods. If you have the RPA file, [Upload the RPA File](/how-do-i/map/upload-the-rpa-file) draws every field at once.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The map is on the wrong place | Location is off on the phone | Pan to the farm, or allow location and tap **My location** |
| Save field is greyed | Fewer than three corners, or no name | Add a corner; type the name |
| The hectares look wrong | A corner is off | Tap the dot to remove it and tap again |
