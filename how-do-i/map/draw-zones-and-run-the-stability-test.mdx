---
title: "Draw Zones and Run the Stability Test"
description: "Draw the parts of a field you treat differently, then test whether they hold year after year."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field, then Zones">
    Open the field's page, then **Zones**. **Draw a zone** draws a polygon inside the boundary. **Zones from satellite** proposes zones from the NDVI capture, for you to accept or discard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the test">
    With two or more zones, the card **Do the zones hold?** appears. **Run the test** asks the satellite archive whether the zones have kept their order, season after season, since 2017.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the verdict">
    **Stable** means the pattern holds and the zones can carry a rate. **Not stable** means they describe this season rather than a pattern. **Too few seasons** means the archive does not yet have three clear ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

A stable field can carry a rate plan and export the file for the tractor. A field that is not stable can hold a plan but cannot export it.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| No card | Fewer than two zones | Draw a second zone |
| Too few seasons | Fewer than three seasons had clear imagery for every zone | Wait a season; the test runs again on its own |
| The test could not run | The satellite archive is not reachable | Try again later; the last verdict stays |
