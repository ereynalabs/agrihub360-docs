---
title: "Draw Zones and Run the Stability Test"
description: "Zones are parts of a field you treat differently. The test says whether they hold year after year."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field, then Zones">
    The field's page, then **Zones**. **Draw a zone** is a polygon inside the boundary; **Zones from satellite** proposes them from the NDVI capture, for you to accept or discard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the test">
    With two or more zones, the card **Do the zones hold?** appears. **Run the test** asks the satellite archive whether the zones have kept their order, season after season, since 2017.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the verdict">
    **Stable**: the pattern holds, the zones can carry a rate. **Not stable**: they describe this season, not a pattern. **Too few seasons**: the archive needs three clear ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

A Stable field can carry a rate plan and export the file for the tractor. A Not stable one can hold a plan but not export it: the verdict is the point.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| No card | Fewer than two zones | Draw a second zone |
| Too few seasons | Fewer than three seasons had clear imagery for every zone | Wait a season; the test runs again on its own |
| The test could not run | The satellite archive is not reachable | Try again later; the last verdict stays |
