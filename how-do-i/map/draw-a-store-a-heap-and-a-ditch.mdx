<Steps>
  <Step title="Map, then Add to the map">
    The list is named as the rules name things, with what you would call it underneath. Stores first: slurry store, silage clamp, field heap, fuel store. Then the water: watercourse, pond, spring or borehole, drain outfall.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the kind and draw it">
    A store is an area: tap around its edge, three points or more. A ditch is a line: tap along its length. A borehole is a point: one tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and add what the rule asks">
    A name, the capacity in cubic metres for a store, and for a field heap the date it was placed: the app works out when it must move. **Add to the map**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The siting check runs the moment both shapes exist: a slurry store 8 m from the ditch reads "SSAFO wants 10 m, two metres short". A store with no water drawn near it says **unchecked**, not compliant. The risk map in the NVZ pack draws the 10 m and 50 m strips from what you drew.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The store says unchecked | No watercourse, pond or borehole is drawn within reach | Draw the nearest water, or note that there is none |
| A heap shows a move-by date in the past | It has been on that site over twelve months | Move it and add the new site with today's date |
| The ditch is piped for that length | The rule measures to open water | Say so in the feature's notes and keep a photo |
