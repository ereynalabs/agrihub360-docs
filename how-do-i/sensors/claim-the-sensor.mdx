**Before you start.** An AgriHub360 Sensor, and a Hub in range and online.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sensors, then Add a sensor">
    **Identify your sensor**: point the camera at the QR code on the sticker, on the underside near the battery door. Or **Type the serial instead**, AH-XXXX-XXXX.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the field">
    The field it stands in. It is drawn at the field's centre until you place it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it on the map">
    The claim ends on **Place it on the map**: the pin where the probe is, the 30 m ring around it. Or do it later from the sensor's page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The sensor is on the Sensors tab with its battery, its signal and its last reading, and on the field as a measured number beside the modelled one.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The serial is refused | It is claimed on another farm | Ask the farm that has it to release it |
| No reading after an hour | The Hub is not online, or out of range | A Sensor Has Gone Quiet |
| The camera does not open | Camera access is off | Type the serial |
