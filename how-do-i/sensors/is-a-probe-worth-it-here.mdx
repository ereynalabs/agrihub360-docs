---
title: "Is a Probe Worth It Here"
description: "Check whether the model is already close enough on a field, or whether a probe would change the numbers."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field, then History">
    The card **Measured against modelled** plots the public model's soil moisture against the nearest measurement over the last two months, with the gap shaded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the verdict">
    On a field with no probe the verdict comes from the nearest COSMOS-UK site. **Keep the model: no probe needed** means the gap is within three points. **Put a probe here** means it is over six. On a field with a probe the card says **Your probe is earning its place**, or that the model would have done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on it">
    **Put a probe here** opens the shop. **Bring a station** opens the bridge for a station you already own. If the verdict is to keep the model, there is nothing to do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The verdict compares the chart's gap with what the field's decisions can tolerate: five points for drilling, three for irrigation.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| No card | No measurement within reach, and no probe | The nearest COSMOS site is too far; the model is all there is |
| The gap changes week to week | Weather | Read the two-month mean, not the day |
| A probe reads wetter than the model | The probe is in a hollow, or the model is dry | Place the probe where the field is typical |
