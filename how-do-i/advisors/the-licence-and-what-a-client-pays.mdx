<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Agronomist page">
    The **Agronomist licence** card: £480 a year, the renewal date, and how many client farms are at a quarter off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get or renew it">
    **Get a licence** opens Stripe's checkout; **Manage in the Stripe portal** for the card and the invoices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What the client sees">
    On each client farm's plans page, the price with your name beside it: Grower at £180 instead of £240 on a farm to 150 ha. The discount ends when the licence does.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

Plans and packs you draft carry your name. Every client farm's legal dates are on your Today.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| A client still pays full price | The licence has lapsed, or you are not their Agronomist | Renew it; check the farm's Team page |
| You advise more farms than the licence shows | Some have not invited you as Agronomist | Ask them to |
