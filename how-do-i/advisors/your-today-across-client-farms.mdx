**Before you start.** A farm has invited you as its Agronomist. [Invite an Advisor](/how-do-i/more/invite-an-advisor) is what the farm does.

<Steps>
  <Step title="More, then Your client farms">
    The Agronomist page shows your licence, the answers waiting for your say, and a section called **Across your farms this week**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the list">
    It uses the same ranking as a farm's Today, run across every farm that shares with you: what the law asks for first, then what closes soonest. Each line carries the farm and the fields as chips.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a farm">
    Tap a chip. The farm's Today opens; a tick there carries your name into that farm's record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

A decision a farm hands to you appears here too, which makes this list a reasonable place to plan the week's visits.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| A farm is missing | It has not shared with you, or you are not its Agronomist | Ask the farm to invite you |
| The list is empty | Nothing on any farm this week | That is the answer |
