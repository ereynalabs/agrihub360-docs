---
title: "Answer a Held Question"
description: "Crop protection questions a grower asks the assistant are held for you. Your answer goes back to them with your name on it."
---

**Before you start.** You are the farm's Agronomist.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Agronomist page">
    The card **Answers waiting for your say** shows how many there are. Tap it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the question and the draft">
    You see the grower's question, the field it is about, and what the assistant would have said. The assistant does not answer crop protection questions itself; it holds them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve, edit or refuse">
    Send the draft as it is, change it, or write your own. The grower sees it in the conversation as **Checked by** you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

Your answer is in the farm's record with your name and the time. A grower who asks the same thing again gets the same answer.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The count does not fall | Another approver took it | It shows who |
| You cannot see a farm's questions | You are not its Agronomist | Ask the farm to invite you |
