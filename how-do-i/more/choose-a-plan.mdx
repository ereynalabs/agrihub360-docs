**Before you start.** The farm is kept, with an email and a password. A guest farm is asked to keep itself first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="More, then Billing">
    The current plan, the seats and sensors in use, and the plans with the price for this farm: the mapped hectares set the band.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the bands">
    **Free**: the map, Today, records, the checklist, every document as a watermarked preview, no hectare cap. **Grower**: £240 a year to 150 ha, £420 to 500 ha, then £1.20 a hectare from £900. With a licensed agronomist on the farm, a quarter less.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose and pay">
    **Choose** on a plan opens Stripe's checkout: the card, then back to the app. Invoices and the card are managed in the Stripe portal from the same page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

Exports unlock at once: the pack downloads clean, share links work, and sensor readings show. Nothing you did on Free is lost, and a downgrade never deletes data.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The page says keep the farm first | This is a guest farm | Keep it with an email and a password, then come back |
| The price is not the band you expected | The mapped hectares fall in a different band | The Map tab's total is the figure; check a field's outline |
| You cannot see the page | Billing is for Admin and Owner | Ask a farm admin |
