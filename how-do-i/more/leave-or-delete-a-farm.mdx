title: "Leave or Delete a Farm"
description: "Leaving takes you off the farm. Deleting removes it for everyone. Exports you downloaded are yours to keep."
---

**Before you start.** Build the pack and download it first. What is deleted is gone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="More, Settings, the farm, then the bottom of the page">
    The **Danger zone**. An owner sees **Delete the farm**; anyone else sees **Leave the farm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave">
    **Leave** takes you off this farm. Its records stay with the people still on it; an owner can invite you back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    **Delete** asks you to confirm. Every field, record, sensor and plan goes, for everyone on it. The subscription ends with it. This cannot be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

Another farm you are on becomes the active one; with none, the app opens on the door to make a new one.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| No Delete | You are not the owner | Ask the owner, or leave instead |
| You want the data first | The pack is the export | Build and download it before deleting |
