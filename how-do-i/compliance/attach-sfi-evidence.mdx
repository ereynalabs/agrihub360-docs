---
title: "Attach SFI Evidence"
description: "Register an SFI action, then capture the evidence that claims it, with the location and the date."
---

**Before you start.** The farm is in England. SFI is an English scheme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Compliance, then SFI actions">
    The page shows the scheme year, the payment expected, and **Register an action** with a chip for each code, CHRW1, CSAM1 and the rest, and the payment per hectare.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register it">
    Pick the code, type the area if it is paid per hectare, **Register action**. It appears under the year with **no evidence** until it has some.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture evidence">
    On the action, tap **Capture evidence** and take a photo with a note. The phone's location and the date go on it. Two or three over the year is what a check asks for.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

**Claim pack** builds the year's evidence as one document per action. The inspection list ticks **SFI action evidence** when every active action has some.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| No SFI actions page | The nation is not England | It does not apply; nothing to do |
| Capture evidence has no point | Location is off | Allow it; a photo without a point is still kept |
| An action is withdrawn | You withdrew it | It stays on the record as withdrawn |
