---
title: "Read the Inspection List and Fix a Line"
description: "The Compliance tab lists what an officer asks to see, in the order they ask. Each line links to where it is fixed."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Compliance">
    The tab opens on **Inspection-ready**, a count such as 8 of 10, and the list. A green tick is held; amber is partly, some fields but not all; red is missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a line">
    An amber or red line says what is missing and where: "a soil analysis under five years on 1 field", "no watercourse, spring or borehole on the map". Tap it to go there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix it and come back">
    Draw the ditch, add the sample or record the crop, whichever the line asked for. The list updates by itself, and so does the count on Today and the pack cover.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The same list is printed on the pack cover as it stood on the day the pack was built. One line, the spreader and sprayer calibration record, is not held in the app and is printed as a paper item to complete by hand.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| Every line is red | The nation is not stated | More, Farm, then the nation; the list re-reads |
| A line will not turn green | One field is still missing it | The line names how many; open each field |
| The count differs from Today | One of them has not refreshed | Pull to refresh |
