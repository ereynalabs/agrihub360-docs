title: "Fill In the NVZ Record"
description: "Every nitrogen application on the field, within seven days, against the crop's N-max."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field, then Nitrogen log">
    At the top, **N-max this year**: what has gone on against the crop's limit, as a bar. Red means over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log an application">
    **Inorganic** or **Organic**, the product, the rate in kg N/ha. **Log application (today)**. For manure, the type and the spreading method give the crop-available nitrogen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the record">
    **Export PDF** prints the field's record in the layout the Regulations describe. The five-year archive is a tap away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The same line appears on the Records tab and in the NVZ record in the pack. The whole-farm limit, 170 kg N/ha of organic nitrogen, is checked on the manure plan.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The bar is red | The crop is over its N-max | It is recorded as it is; the plan on the nutrient page says what the crop needed |
| The product is not in the list | Type it; the list is a help, not a rule | Type the name and the rate |
| The log wants a crop | The field has none | What is growing, then plant a variety |
