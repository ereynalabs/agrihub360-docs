title: "Build and Share the Pack"
description: "The compliance pack for the year, and a link an inspector can open without an account."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the checklist first">
    The Compliance tab opens on the inspection-ready checklist. Each item is a tap from where it is fixed. What is missing here prints as missing on the pack's cover, so fix what you can before you build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the pack">
    Under the checklist, the pack card shows its cover. **Build the 2026 pack** downloads a zip: the cover, then each document in the layout its regime uses, then the machine-readable files. On the free plan the same button opens a preview of every page, watermarked, with the price to unlock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    **Share link for an inspector or agronomist** makes a link that downloads the real pack. It needs no account and stops working after the days you set, 14 by default and 90 at most. Send it however you send things.
  </Step>
</Steps>
