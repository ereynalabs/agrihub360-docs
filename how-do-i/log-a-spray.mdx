title: "Log a Spray"
description: "Two minutes in the field, with or without signal, and the entry is a line on the plant protection product record."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press + and choose Spray">
    The **+** button sits on every tab. The sheet opens on Spray with the field, the date and the weather already filled in. Change the field with its chip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the product">
    Start typing the name. The matches come from the MAPP register with their approval for the field's crop. Pick one: the MAPP number and the check come with it. A withdrawn product cannot be picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate and target">
    The rate in litres or kilograms per hectare, and the target if you know it. The four assurance fields under them are optional: water volume, growth stage, LERAP category and buffer, harvest interval. A blank prints as "not recorded" on the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A photo, then Save">
    **Add a photo** opens the camera; a picture of the label is enough. **Save record** writes the entry. With no signal it is kept on the phone and sent when you are back in range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The spray is a row on the Records tab under today's date, with its MAPP number, and a line on the [plant protection product record](/features/compliance-and-records/spray-log-with-mapp-check) in the pack. A product the register flags is saved with the warning on it; a blocked product is not saved, and the sheet says why.
