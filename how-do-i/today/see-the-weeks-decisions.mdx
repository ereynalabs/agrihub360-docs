<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Today">
    Today is the first tab. The farm's fields sit at the top in their crop colours on the satellite ground, and under them is the week's decisions as one list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the order">
    What the law asks for comes first, such as a closed period that starts, a limit, or a store that fails a rule. Then comes what closes soonest, such as a spray or drilling window. Then a reading that crossed a line you set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read one line">
    Each decision is a sentence with the field, the date and the rule. The same rule on three fields is one line with three field chips. Tap a chip to open that field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The list rebuilds every time Today opens. A decision that no longer applies drops off; one you ticked comes back if the rule fires again next week. Under the list sits the inspection-ready meter, the same list the Compliance tab and the pack cover carry.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| Today is empty | No field has a crop, so no rule applies yet | Map, then the field, then **What is growing** |
| A decision you expect is missing | The nation is not stated, so the scheme rows are off | More, Settings, the farm, then the nation |
| The same field appears three times | Three different rules fire on it | Read the rule under each line; they are three jobs |
