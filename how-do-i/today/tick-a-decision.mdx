title: "Tick a Decision"
description: "A tick marks the week's decision done, with your name on it."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the box on the line">
    The box on the left of each decision. It fills, the line greys, and the row says who ticked it and when.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Undo by tapping again">
    A second tap clears it. Nothing else changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it come back">
    A tick is for the week. If the rule fires again, the decision returns with an empty box.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

On a farm with more than one person the row reads "Ticked by Rhys, 2 h ago". The tick is in the farm's record with the name, which is what an assessor asks about a job that was done.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The tick does not fill | No signal | It is queued and sends when the phone is back online |
| The line came back | The rule fired again | Read the date; it is a new week or a new field |
