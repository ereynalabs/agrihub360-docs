---
title: "Tick a Decision"
description: "Mark a decision on Today as done for the week. The tick carries your name."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the box on the line">
    Tap the box on the left of the decision. It fills in, the line goes grey, and the row says who ticked it and when.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Undo by tapping again">
    A second tap clears it. Nothing else changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it come back">
    A tick lasts for the week. If the rule fires again, the decision returns with an empty box.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

On a farm with more than one person the row reads "Ticked by Rhys, 2 h ago". The tick is kept in the farm's record with the name and the time.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The tick does not fill | No signal | It is queued and sends when the phone is back online |
| The line came back | The rule fired again | Read the date; it is a new week or a new field |
