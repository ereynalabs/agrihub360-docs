<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Key dates">
    Today, then **Key dates** at the top of the list, or More, then Key dates. Every closed period, heap deadline and cutting period on the farm, worked out from each field's crop and soil.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the weekly email">
    More, Profile, **What to tell me about**. **Key dates and the week ahead** sends the next fortnight's dates every Monday and a summary of overnight events each morning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the push">
    On the same page, **An alert on a field fires, or a sensor goes quiet** sends a push to this phone as it happens. **Save notifications**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

A date on Key dates is on Today the week it falls. The email carries the same dates with the same regulations, so a farm without the phone to hand is still told.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| No dates | No field has a crop, or the nation is not stated | Give each field its crop; More, Farm, the nation |
| No push arrives | This phone is not registered, or pushes are off in its settings | Sign in on the phone once; check its notification settings |
| The email is missing | Not turned on, or in spam | What to tell me about, then look for agrihub360.com |
