title: "Read Where a Decision Comes From"
description: "Every line on Today names its source: the regulation, the model, or the device."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the line under the sentence">
    Under each decision, in small type with an information mark, is the source. A closed period names the regulation; a window names the model and its inputs; a reading names the device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the source">
    The regulation opens on gov.uk. A model opens its card on the field with the inputs it used. A device opens its page with the last reading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check measured or modelled">
    A number worked out by the app says **modelled** and names the model. A number read by a device says **measured** and names the device and the time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

Nothing on Today is unexplained. If a line says modelled and you want measured, [Is a Probe Worth It Here](/how-do-i/sensors/is-a-probe-worth-it-here) tells you whether a sensor would change the number.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The source says "nation not stated" | The nation is missing | More, Settings, the farm, then the nation |
| The model names a field with no probe | The reading is modelled from public data | That is the honest answer; a probe replaces it |
