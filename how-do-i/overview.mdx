Each page in this group is one thing you do in the app, in the order you tap. The screens are the app on the demo farm, Whitaker Farm, at phone size. What a feature is and why it exists lives in the feature groups below; these pages only say how.

| Task | Page |
|---|---|
| Write a spray so it lands on the plant protection product record | [Log a Spray](/how-do-i/log-a-spray) |
| Bring an Ecowitt station you already own | [Connect a Station](/how-do-i/connect-a-station) |
| Build the compliance pack and send it to an inspector | [Build and Share the Pack](/how-do-i/build-and-share-the-pack) |
| Put a herd on a field, or house it | [Move a Herd](/how-do-i/move-a-herd) |
| Work out why a sensor has stopped reporting | [A Sensor Has Gone Quiet](/how-do-i/a-sensor-has-gone-quiet) |

More tasks follow the same shape: draw a field, upload the RPA file, keep a guest farm, add a photo to a record, set a trigger, share the pack, invite an advisor.
