---
title: "How Do I"
description: "One task per page, three to five steps, the real screen beside them, grouped by the tab your thumb is on."
---

Each page in this group is one thing you do in the app, in the order you tap. The screens are the app on the demo farms, Whitaker Farm and Cwm Elan Farm, at phone size. Every page ends with what you see next and a table of what to do if it does not. What a feature is and why it exists lives in the groups below these.

## Today

| Task | What you get |
|---|---|
| [See the Week's Decisions](/how-do-i/today/see-the-weeks-decisions) | Today is the front page. One list, ranked, with a tick on each line. |
| [Tick a Decision](/how-do-i/today/tick-a-decision) | A tick marks the week's decision done, with your name on it. |
| [Hand a Decision to Someone](/how-do-i/today/hand-a-decision-to-someone) | One minute. The tick then carries their name, and they get a push. |
| [Read Where a Decision Comes From](/how-do-i/today/read-where-a-decision-comes-from) | Every line on Today names its source: the regulation, the model, or the device. |
| [Get the Email and the Push Before a Date](/how-do-i/today/get-the-email-and-the-push-before-a-date) | The key dates come by email on Monday; alerts come as a push as they happen. |

## Map

| Task | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Draw a Field](/how-do-i/map/draw-a-field) | Three taps on the map and a name. The area is worked out for you. |
| [Upload the RPA File](/how-do-i/map/upload-the-rpa-file) | Every field on the map from the file the Rural Payments service already gave you. |
| [Draw a Store, a Heap and a Ditch](/how-do-i/map/draw-a-store-a-heap-and-a-ditch) | Two shapes and the app measures the distance the rule wants. |
| [Place a Probe on the Field](/how-do-i/map/place-a-probe-on-the-field) | A sensor is a point. Put the pin where the probe stands, and the ring shows what it speaks for. |
| [Draw Zones and Run the Stability Test](/how-do-i/map/draw-zones-and-run-the-stability-test) | Zones are parts of a field you treat differently. The test says whether they hold year after year. |
| [Set the Rates and Export for the Tractor](/how-do-i/map/set-the-rates-and-export-for-the-tractor) | A nitrogen rate per zone, then the file the terminal reads. Only on a field whose zones hold. |
| [Move a Herd](/how-do-i/map/move-a-herd) | Put a herd on a field or house it; the manure plan and the field's record follow. |
| [Read the Grazing Wedge](/how-do-i/map/read-the-grazing-wedge) | Every field's grass cover as a bar, from the satellite, with the target and the residual drawn across. |
| [Switch the Layers](/how-do-i/map/switch-the-layers) | Fields, stores, water, herds, sensors, the satellite ground and the vegetation tile, each a chip. |

## Records

| Task | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Log a Spray](/how-do-i/records/log-a-spray) | Two minutes in the field, with or without signal, and the entry is a line on the plant protection product record. |
| [Log Fertiliser or Manure](/how-do-i/records/log-fertiliser-or-manure) | A spread is a line on the nitrogen record within the seven days the Regulations allow. |
| [Add a Photo to a Record](/how-do-i/records/add-a-photo-to-a-record) | A picture of the label or the load turns a note into evidence. |
| [Let the Assistant Draft a Record](/how-do-i/records/let-the-assistant-draft-a-record) | Tell the assistant what you did. It fills in the sheet; you press Save. |
| [Log a Soil Sample](/how-do-i/records/log-a-soil-sample) | The lab's P, K, Mg and pH on the field. The nutrient plan rests on it. |
| [Record a Harvest](/how-do-i/records/record-a-harvest) | A harvest attaches to the planting: the yield, the moisture, the date. |
| [Write a Record with No Signal](/how-do-i/records/write-a-record-with-no-signal) | The record is kept on the phone and sent when you are back in range. You do nothing different. |

## Compliance

| Task | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Read the Inspection List and Fix a Line](/how-do-i/compliance/read-the-inspection-list-and-fix-a-line) | What an officer asks to see, in the order they ask. Each line is a tap from where it is fixed. |
| [See the Closed Period on Each Field](/how-do-i/compliance/see-the-closed-period-on-each-field) | The dates you must not spread, worked out from the nation, the soil and the crop on each field. |
| [Build and Share the Pack](/how-do-i/compliance/build-and-share-the-pack) | The compliance pack for the year, and a link an inspector can open without an account. |
| [Fill In the NVZ Record](/how-do-i/compliance/fill-in-the-nvz-record) | Every nitrogen application on the field, within seven days, against the crop's N-max. |
| [Attach SFI Evidence](/how-do-i/compliance/attach-sfi-evidence) | Register the action, then capture the evidence that claims it, with the point and the date. |
| [Open the Manure Plan](/how-do-i/compliance/open-the-manure-plan) | The manure management plan, worked out from the herds, the stores and the fields. Nothing is typed twice. |

## More

| Task | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Set Up the Farm](/how-do-i/more/set-up-the-farm) | The name, the nation, the holding and the business. What an inspector asks for first is on every document's masthead. |
| [Invite the Team and Set Roles](/how-do-i/more/invite-the-team-and-set-roles) | An email and a role. They get a link that works for seven days. |
| [Invite an Advisor](/how-do-i/more/invite-an-advisor) | Your agronomist reads the farm and writes recommendations. They do not edit it. |
| [Choose a Plan](/how-do-i/more/choose-a-plan) | Everything on screen is free. The pack, its share links and sensor readings are the paid step, priced by the farm's size. |
| [Turn On Two-factor](/how-do-i/more/turn-on-two-factor) | An authenticator app on top of the password. Emailed codes stay as the fallback. |
| [Leave or Delete a Farm](/how-do-i/more/leave-or-delete-a-farm) | Leaving takes you off the farm. Deleting removes it for everyone. Exports you downloaded are yours to keep. |

## Sensors

| Task | What you get |
|---|---|
| [Is a Probe Worth It Here](/how-do-i/sensors/is-a-probe-worth-it-here) | The app tells you where the model is already good enough, and where a probe would pay. Sometimes the answer is no. |
| [Claim the Sensor](/how-do-i/sensors/claim-the-sensor) | Scan the sticker, choose the field, place it. Three steps. |
| [Connect a Station](/how-do-i/sensors/connect-a-station) | An Ecowitt station you already own posts its readings through its own gateway. Four lines to type. |
| [Set a Trigger](/how-do-i/sensors/set-a-trigger) | A reading crossing a line, a sensor going quiet, frost in the forecast. A push, and a webhook if you want one. |
| [A Sensor Has Gone Quiet](/how-do-i/sensors/a-sensor-has-gone-quiet) | What the Sensors tab says when a device stops reporting, what it means, and what to do in the order that finds the fault fastest. |
