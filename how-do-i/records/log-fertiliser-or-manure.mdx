<Steps>
  <Step title="Press + and choose the kind">
    The **+** button sits on every tab. **Fertiliser or other** is for P, K, lime, irrigation and foliar feeds. **Nitrogen (NVZ)** is for anything with nitrogen in it, manufactured or manure: that one lands on the NVZ record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The field, the product, the rate">
    Pick the field chip. Type the product. For nitrogen, choose **Manufactured** or **Slurry or manure**, then the rate in kg N/ha; the sheet works out the total from the field's area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A photo, then Save">
    **Add a photo** of the bag or the load. **Save record**. With no signal it is kept on the phone and sent later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The entry is a row on the Records tab and a line on the field's nitrogen log, counted against the crop's N-max. A manure entry carries its readily available nitrogen for the closed-period rules.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The sheet refuses the date | It is more than seven days ago | The Regulations want the record within the week; note the true date in Notes |
| The N-max bar went red | This spread takes the crop over its limit | Read the field's nitrogen log; the plan sets the limit |
| The total looks wrong | The field's hectares are off | Fix them on the field; the total follows |
