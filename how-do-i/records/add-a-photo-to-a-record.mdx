---
title: "Add a Photo to a Record"
description: "A picture of the label or the load turns a note into evidence."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the sheet, Add a photo">
    Every kind on the + sheet has **Add a photo** above Save. It opens the camera; the label, the bag or the heap is enough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Save record**. The photo goes with it. If the record saves and the photo does not, the row says so and you can add it again from the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the mark on the row">
    On the Records tab each row carries its grade. **Evidence: in the field** means it was written within the day, on the field, with a photo or a reading. **Typed later** means it was not.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The pack cover counts them: "Records as evidence, 12 of 15". Nothing is refused; a record typed on Sunday night is still a record, and the mark says so honestly, which is what an assessor reads as a good sign.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| The camera does not open | Camera access is off for the app | Allow it in the phone's settings |
| The mark says Typed later though you were there | Location was off, so the phone's point was not saved | Allow location; the next record carries it |
| The photo did not go on | No signal when it saved | Open the record and add it again |
