---
title: "Write a Record with No Signal"
description: "The record is kept on the phone and sent when you are back in range. You do nothing different."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write it as usual">
    Press **+**, fill in the sheet, **Save record**. The toast reads **Saved, will send**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Carry on">
    The row is on the Records tab straight away with a small queued mark. The time and the phone's point are the time and point you saved, not the time it sends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back in range">
    The queue sends on its own. A record that could not send keeps its mark and says why when you tap it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You See Next

The evidence grade uses the time you saved, so a record written in the field with no signal still reads **Evidence: in the field**.

## If It Does Not

| You see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| A row stays queued for a day | The phone has not been online, or the app has not been opened | Open the app on wifi |
| The row says it was refused | The field or the product no longer exists | Tap it, fix it, save again |
