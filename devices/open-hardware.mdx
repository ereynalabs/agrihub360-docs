The AgriHub360 Sensor is built from commodity modules and nothing inside is potted or glued shut. A part that fails can be replaced, and the sensor keeps its device id, its key and its field history.

## Firmware

The firmware reads the probe, posts through the [Hub](/devices/hub) every fifteen minutes or slower, and sleeps between readings. A reflashed board keeps the sensor's identity.
