## What It Tells You

A **pest forecast** says when the weather has favoured a pest or a disease on a field, before symptoms show. Aphids develop with temperature, so the [BYDV](/models/bydv) count runs on degree-days. Other pests and diseases are forecast by AgriHub360 agronomy staff from published monitoring, with a pressure level, a confidence, a recommended action and an expiry date. A forecast describes conditions. It says infection was favoured. It does not say the crop has the disease.

## How It Works

BYDV sums degree-days above 3 °C from emergence and writes a row per field per day. Every other forecast is a row entered by staff against a field: the pest, its category, the expected emergence, the pressure level, the confidence and the action. A forecast expires on its own date. The field card shows the latest active forecast, or the reason when the BYDV model cannot answer.
