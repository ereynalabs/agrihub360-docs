## What It Tells You

A **scout photo diagnosis** is the most likely disease or pest on a leaf you photographed in the field. It carries a confidence score and what the model saw. A lesion you are unsure about becomes a named call on the field's timeline. The confidence is the model's own and is indicative. A low score is a reason to walk the patch again. It is not a reason to spray, and the app names no product: what to spray is a decision for a qualified person, and the diagnosis goes to your agronomist for that. The diagnosis comes from the [Scout Photo Diagnosis](/models/scout-diagnosis) model.

## How It Works

The photo is stripped of location data and downscaled. It is sent to a vision model constrained to a curated list of UK arable diseases and pests. A reply that does not fit the schema, or whose confidence is below the 0.30 floor, is recorded as unknown. The name comes from the list. The model supplies only the match. A diagnosis below 0.6, or one you correct, goes to the agronomist queue first; the verdict says what it does not know rather than filling the gap.
