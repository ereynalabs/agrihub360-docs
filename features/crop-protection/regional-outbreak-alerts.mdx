## What It Tells You

A **regional outbreak alert** says that an outbreak has been reported or is spreading in your region. It describes the region. It does not describe your field. Each alert carries its source, such as AHDB regional monitoring, its confidence, and the radius it applies to, and it is never shown as a per-field result. An alert is the prompt to check your own crop's stage and walk your own fields. If they agree, plan the pass.

## How It Works

Alerts are raised by AgriHub360 agronomy staff from published monitoring and scoped to a region and a radius. Every alert carries its source and its confidence. A farm with fields inside the radius sees the alert and how many of its fields fall inside. The notification says "in your region". It never says "your field".
