Account & Data covers who can see the farm record and what leaves it. Each sharing switch is off by default. Sign-in supports two-factor authentication. A sharing group pools readings with the farms you choose.

A sharing switch is set per farm. Two-factor sign-in is set per account. A sharing group is a list of farms you name. An entry made without signal waits on the phone and is sent when it reconnects.

## What's Included

Status is graded against the app as it is today. **Live** means built and working in the app. **In build** means being built and not on farms yet.

| Feature | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Consent-first Data Sharing](/features/account-and-data/consent-first-data-sharing) | Scout photos and harvest results are separate switches, off by default | Live |
| [Two-factor Sign-in](/features/account-and-data/two-factor-sign-in) | TOTP on any account | Live |
| [Farmer Sharing Groups](/features/account-and-data/farmer-sharing-groups) | Pool readings with the farms you choose | Live |
| [Offline Action Queue](/features/account-and-data/offline-action-queue) | Entries made without signal are sent when the phone reconnects | Live |
