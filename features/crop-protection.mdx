Crop Protection covers what is attacking the crop and when it is likely to. Photograph a leaf in the field and the app returns a diagnosis with a confidence score. An agronomist checks every diagnosis before it becomes advice.

Pest forecasts run on degree-days for UK crops. Regional outbreak alerts are scoped to your area and carry their source and confidence.

A photo passes a vision model and then an agronomist. The weather drives the pest forecasts and the regional alerts on the field.

## What's Included

| Feature | What it does |

| [Pest Forecasts](/features/crop-protection/pest-forecasts) | Degree-day risk windows, specific to UK crops |
| [Regional Outbreak Alerts](/features/crop-protection/regional-outbreak-alerts) | Geo-scoped, with source and confidence on every alert |
