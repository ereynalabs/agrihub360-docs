## What It Tells You

**Trafficability** is whether the field will carry a sprayer or a spreader without rutting or compaction. On heavy soil it decides whether a pass is possible.

## How It Works

The window runs on the field's readings and weather, like the other windows. It reports on the field card with the reading behind it.
