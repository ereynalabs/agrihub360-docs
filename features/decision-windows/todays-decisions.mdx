## What It Tells You

**Today** is the app's front page. It lists the decisions the farm faces this week as one ranked list: a closed period that starts on a field, a spray or drilling window that is open, a siting rule a store fails, a reading that crossed a threshold. Each decision is a sentence with the field, the date and the rule behind it, and a tick.

The order is fixed. What the law asks for comes first, then what closes soonest. A closed period for slurry outranks a spray window because missing one is an offence and missing the other is a lost afternoon.

## How It Works

The list is built every time Today opens, from the key dates, the decision windows, the siting checks and the alerts. The same rule on several fields is one decision with the fields as chips, so three spray windows read as one line. A tick marks the decision done for the week; it comes back if the rule fires again.

| Band | Holds | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Legal | A key date, a limit, a siting failure | The closed period for high readily available N manure starts on North Pasture on 1 October |
| Window | A decision window that is open or closing | Spray window on three fields, Saturday 10:00 to Sunday 00:00 |
| Reading | A custom trigger that crossed | Soil moisture on North Pasture fell to 15 % |
| Insight | A model's advisory | The nitrogen from the soil this month is about 9 kg N/ha |

Under the list sits the [inspection-ready](/features/compliance-and-records/one-compliance-vault) meter, the same checklist the Compliance tab and the pack's cover carry, and three tiles: the weather for the largest field, the sensors reporting, and the nearest outbreak. The farm's fields sit at the top of the page, in their crop colours on the satellite ground; a tap opens the Map.

Every decision folds its source behind an information mark: the regulation for a key date, the model and its inputs for a window, the device and the reading for a trigger.
