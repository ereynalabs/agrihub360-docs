Four regimes ask a farm for a written record: the nitrogen rules, the spray rules, the payment scheme and the deforestation rule on exports to Europe. Each names what to write, when to write it, how long to keep it and who will read it. The pages in this group set out each law in plain words, show where the same duty appears in the EU and the United States, and say what the app keeps for it.

Nitrogen applications are logged within seven days. Every spray product is checked against the MAPP register at entry. SFI actions carry their evidence. The compliance pack bundles all of it for an inspection, by nation. A section with nothing in it says "nothing recorded".

## The Regimes

One row per regime and jurisdiction. Each page in the group carries the full rule.

| Regime | Where | Legal basis | What it asks you to keep | Who inspects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen records | England | The Nitrate Pollution Prevention Regulations 2015 in NVZs [1]; the Farming Rules for Water on every farm [3] | A nitrogen plan for each crop, every application recorded within one week, kept five years, within 170 kg N/ha and N max and outside the closed periods [2]; a soil test no more than five years old | Environment Agency [1][3] |
| Nitrogen records | Wales | The Water Resources (Control of Agricultural Pollution) (Wales) Regulations 2021 [10] | The same duties on every farm in Wales, kept five years | Natural Resources Wales [10] |
| Nitrogen records | Scotland | The Action Programme for Nitrate Vulnerable Zones (Scotland) Regulations 2008 [11] | A fertiliser and manure plan and records in five NVZs, kept five years | Scottish Government Rural Payments and Inspections Division [12] |
| Nitrogen records | Northern Ireland | The Nutrient Action Programme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 [13] | Records for the whole territory, prepared by 30 June each year and kept five years | DAERA [13][14] |
| Nitrogen records | EU member states | Council Directive 91/676/EEC and each state's action programme [15] | 170 kg N/ha from livestock manure, closed periods and records in the programme area, with dates and deadlines set nationally | The national authority; the Commission receives a report every four years [15] |
| Nitrogen records | United States | State nutrient management laws built on NRCS Conservation Practice Standard 590, for example Maryland, Delaware and Wisconsin [16][17][18] | A written nutrient management plan, soil tests on a cycle, application records and, in some states, an annual report | State departments of agriculture or natural resources [16][17][18] |
| Spray records | Great Britain | Retained Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009 Article 67 [5]; SI 2012/1657 [4]; SI 2020/552 [6] | Product, time, dose, area and crop for every professional application, kept at least three years; a certificate; a tested sprayer; registration | HSE, through its Chemicals Regulation Division [7] |
| Spray records | EU member states | Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009 Article 67; Directive 2009/128/EC [19][20] | The same five-item record kept three years; training, equipment inspection and integrated pest management under a national action plan | The national competent authority [20] |
| Spray records | United States | EPA Worker Protection Standard, 40 CFR part 170 [21][22]; USDA restricted use pesticide records, 7 CFR part 110 [23][24] | Application details displayed within 24 hours and kept two years; restricted use applications recorded within 14 days and kept two years | State agencies for the WPS; USDA and state representatives for restricted use records [22][24] |
| Payment on evidence | England | SFI26 scheme rules [8] | Evidence for each action at land parcel level, kept seven years from the agreement end date, with an annual declaration | Rural Payments Agency [8] |
| Payment on evidence | EU member states | CAP eco-schemes in each state's CAP strategic plan [25] | Proof of the practice claimed in each year | The member state's paying agency [25] |
| Payment on evidence | United States | Conservation Stewardship Program and Environmental Quality Incentives Program [26] | Proof of the conservation activities under a five-year CSP contract or an EQIP contract | USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service [26] |
| Deforestation-free supply | Any farm selling into the EU | Regulation (EU) 2023/1115, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2025/2650 [9][27] | Parcel geolocation and dates showing the product does not come from land deforested after the cut-off, given in a due diligence statement or a simplified declaration | The EU operator placing the product, and its competent authority [9] |
