**Variety evidence** is the harvest record for the field's microclimate: how each variety has yielded in fields like this one, with sample sizes, a margin and a confidence tier, and a ranking only where the numbers can carry one. It is built from harvests farms have recorded, never from trial data or a score.

## What It Tells You

For the field's cluster: each variety with its observations, farms, seasons, mean yield in kg/ha, margin, and a tier of insufficient, low, moderate or good. A note says whether the list is ranked or only shown, and why. A count of results withheld because their farm hasn't opted into sharing.

## How It's Calculated

An **outcome** is one harvest with a yield per hectare, on a field that has a microclimate cluster, attributed to the variety in its planting record. Its season is the harvest year.

Per variety in the cluster: observations is the number of outcomes; farms is the number of distinct farms among them; the mean yield is rounded to the nearest kg/ha; the margin is 1.96 times the standard deviation divided by the square root of the observations, reported only from three observations up.

Tier: insufficient below three observations; low at three or more observations on fewer than three farms; moderate at three or more farms; good at six or more farms. The thresholds count farms, not observations, because three seasons on one farm hold soil, management and machinery constant.

Ranking rule: varieties with a tier above insufficient are ordered by mean yield, and a ranking is shown only when at least two of them clear the bar. With one or none, every variety is listed unranked with its sample size and the note says so. A field with no cluster gets no comparison at all, and the note says the profile builds from sensor readings over time.

Worked example. A variety in the field's cluster with five recorded outcomes on three farms: 8,100, 8,600, 7,900, 9,000 and 8,400 kg/ha.

| Figure | Working | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Observations | Five harvests with a yield per hectare | 5 |
| Farms | Three distinct farms | 3 |
| Mean yield | 42,000 over 5 | 8,400 kg/ha |
| Margin | Standard deviation 430; 1.96 times 430 over the square root of 5 | plus or minus 377 kg/ha |
| Tier | 5 observations on 3 farms | Moderate |

## Where The Numbers Come From

| Input | Record | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Yield per hectare, harvest date | The harvest record | Measured value, entered by the farm |
| Variety | The planting the harvest belongs to | Record |
| Cluster | The field's microclimate profile, from median soil temperature and moisture readings | Modelled value from readings |
| Sharing | The farm's outcome-sharing opt-in | Consent |

Your own results always count for you. Another farm's result counts only where that farm has opted in, and the withheld count is shown so a small sample is explained. Confidence is a tier word, never a percentage. The running mean and standard deviation per variety and cluster are updated each time a harvest with a yield per hectare is recorded on a field with a cluster.

## Limits

- The margin is a plain standard error. Results from one farm are correlated, so the tier is the caveat, not the margin.
- A mean is shown from a single harvest. A margin and a ranking are not.
- A harvest recorded without a yield per hectare, or on a field with no cluster yet, doesn't become an outcome.
- Ranking is by mean yield alone. Quality grade, moisture at harvest and disease notes are recorded but not ranked on.
- The evidence covers the cluster the field is in now. If the profile moves to another cluster, the comparison set moves with it.
