Variety Selection ranks varieties on the field's own microclimate and shows the evidence behind each ranking.

The recommender uses the AHDB Recommended List and the field's season of readings. Every recommendation shows why it was made.

## What's Included

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Variety Recommender](/features/farm-management/variety-recommender) | AHDB Recommended List calibrated to your microclimate, confidence-banded |
| [Variety Evidence](/features/farm-management/variety-evidence) | The readings and Recommended List entries behind each ranking |
