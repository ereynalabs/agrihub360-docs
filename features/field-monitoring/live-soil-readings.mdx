## What It Tells You

A **live soil reading** is a measured value from a probe in the field: soil moisture as a percentage and soil temperature in °C, with the device and the time it came from. Soil moisture tells you whether the crop can take up nitrogen this week and whether the ground will carry a spreader. Soil temperature tells you whether the seedbed is warm enough to drill and whether the soil is warm enough for an application. The same readings feed the [drilling window](/models/drilling-window), [nitrogen timing](/models/nitrogen-timing) and [water stress](/models/water-status) models. A field with a probe gets those answers from its own soil.

## How It Works

An [AgriHub360 Sensor](/devices/sensor) reports every 15 minutes or slower over LoRa to the Hub. The Hub posts each reading to the Device API. A [device you bring](/devices/byod) or a [weather station you already own](/devices/weather-station) posts to the same place. Each reading is labelled **measured** and shown on the field timeline next to the modelled value it replaces.

The field page sets the two soil readings as figures, with the week's shape under each and a red figure when the reading is out of the crop's range. The Sensors tab lists every device with its last reading, how long ago it came, and the day's sparkline. A sensor that stops reporting is shown as stale, and a card says what to do: restart the gateway, check the probe's battery, or check the station's upload settings. A silent gateway takes its sensors' silence with it, so it is one card, not five.
