## What It Tells You

**Consent-first sharing** means nothing about your farm leaves it unless you turn a switch on. There are two switches per field: scout photos, which train the disease models, and harvest results, which feed the variety evidence across farms. Each is off by default. You can share harvest results with a buyer and keep every photo private. You can turn any switch off again.

## How It Works

Each switch is set per farm in settings. A switch that is off keeps that data inside the farm. A switch that is on shares that data and nothing else.
