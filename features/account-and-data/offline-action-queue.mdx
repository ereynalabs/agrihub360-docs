## What It Tells You

The **offline action queue** lets you record an application in a field with no signal. The entry reaches the record when the phone reconnects. The NVZ rule asks for a nitrogen application to be logged within seven days. The queue logs it on the date you did it.

## How It Works

A record written without signal shows on the Records timeline at once, marked as pending, and becomes a plain row when it lands. A photo taken with it is kept on the phone and sent with it. The queue replays when the phone reconnects and when the app comes back to the foreground; each entry is sent once, with the time it was written.

An action taken without signal is held on the phone and sent when it reconnects. The record stores the date and time you entered it.
