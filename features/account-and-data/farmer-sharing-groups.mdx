## What It Tells You

A **sharing group** is a set of farms that agree to see each other's readings. A neighbour's soil temperature on the far side of the valley is a reading your own probe cannot give.

## How It Works

You choose the farms in the group. Only readings are shared. A farm outside the group sees nothing.
